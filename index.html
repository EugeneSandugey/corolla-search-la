Where to get it inspected — near Van Nuys 91406

Yes, this is a normal thing to ask a shop for. It is called a pre-purchase inspection (PPI). You drive the car there, or meet the seller there, and the shop puts it on a lift for about an hour. Around $100–$200 for a Corolla. Book it by phone and say the words “pre-purchase inspection” — they will know exactly what you mean. If a seller will not let you take it to a shop, that is the end of that car.

What to say when you call

“Hi — I’m looking at a used Toyota Corolla from a private seller. Do you do pre-purchase inspections, what do you charge, and how soon could you fit one in?”

Then ask two follow-ups that matter: do I get a written report, and do you put it on a lift. A shop that says yes to both is doing the real version. Book the car in your name, not the seller’s — whoever the shop thinks is the customer is who they report to.
